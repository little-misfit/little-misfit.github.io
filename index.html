Xuanpu Zhang

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. at Tianjin University and expect to graduate in 2029. I received my M.S. degree in computer vision from Tianjin University, advised by Prof. Dan Song, and my B.S. degree in Electronic Information from Hebei University of Technology, where I worked with Prof. Zhenxv Bai.

My research interests lie in visual content generation, with a current focus on video models and world models. Previously, I worked on image generation, image editing, and virtual try-on.

I am currently a research intern at Kling AI Research. Before that, I interned at Kuaishou Technology until July 2026 and at Alibaba in 2024.
